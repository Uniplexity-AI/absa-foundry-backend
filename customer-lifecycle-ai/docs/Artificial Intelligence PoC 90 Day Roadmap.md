**Outcome Table For PoC – 3 Month Projection**

| **Workstream** | **Deliverables** | **Details / Outcome** | **Timeline** |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data Access &amp; Compliance | Data integration completed, Compliance review completed | Secure access to anonymised customer, transactional, and product data. Data access aligned with Absa's governance, privacy, and security requirements. | Month 1 |  |
| Business Discovery & Use Case   Design | Customer Lifecycle framework, Customer Value framework, KPI baseline established | Define customer lifecycle stages, customer value methodology, business   objectives, success measures, and model requirements. | Month 1 |  |
| Customer Lifecycle Prediction Development | Lifecycle segmentation model, Churn prediction model, Next-best-action engine | AI model developed to identify customer lifecycle stages, predict churn risk, and recommend proactive engagement actions to Relationship Managers. | Month 1–2 |  |
| Customer Value &amp; Balance Intelligence Development | Customer value scoring model, Balance growth prediction model, Opportunity recommendation engine | AI model developed to identify future high-value customers, forecast balance growth, and detect potential balance erosion risks. | Month 1–2 |  |
| Model Validation &amp; Optimisation | Feature engineering, Model tuning, Explainability assessment | Improve model accuracy, interpretability, and business usability. Feature importance and model drivers documented for transparency and governance. | Month 2–3 |  |
| Dashboard Development | Dashboard v1 Wireframe, Interactive Dashboard v2 | Development of AI dashboards providing lifecycle insights, churn risk monitoring, customer value scoring, balance forecasts, and opportunity recommendations. | Month 1–3 |  |
| Stakeholder Engagement | Business workshops, Demonstration sessions, Feedback incorporation | Regular engagement with Relationship Managers, Product Teams, CRM Teams, and Executives to validate outputs and refine dashboards. | Month 2–3 |  |
| Pilot Testing &amp; Evaluation | User acceptance testing, Business validation, KPI assessment | Validate model performance against agreed business outcomes and test usability with selected business users. | Month 3 |  |
| Documentation &amp; Knowledge Transfer | Technical documentation, User guide, Knowledge transfer workshop | Documentation of models, dashboards, methodologies, and operational procedures to support future scaling. | Month 3 |  |
| Final PoC Report &amp; Roadmap | Executive summary, Business case, Production roadmap | Presentation of business value achieved, lessons learned, ROI opportunities, risks, and recommendations for enterprise deployment. | End of Month 3 |  |
